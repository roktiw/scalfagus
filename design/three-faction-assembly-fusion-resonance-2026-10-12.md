## Canon: same strategic problem, three different ontologies

| Dimension | MAN — mechanical composition | ORG — irreversible biological assimilation | ETH — meditative and energetic union |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner-selected method | Multiple machines physically mount/lock into a **meta-machine**; single machines physically change mode | Two or more organisms **permanently fuse**, or organism **devours / absorbs** other organisms for growth; mutation goes forward | Existing beings **energetically combine into one more complex being**, or **meditate and connect into a new collective system** |
| What happens to participating identity? | Original \`machine_id\` and source actors remain real subordinate parts in an assembly | Original biological units **cease independent existence** as irreversible sources; resulting entity has new identity with lineage ledger | For **meditative connection**, retain actual participants as network nodes; for **deep merger**, complex resulting energetic identity/participant memory mapping are **not yet owner-defined** |
| Detaching / returning | Structurally possible on legal terrain and with real components intact; costs and destruction still apply | **No simple inverse**: do not restore two full precursor organisms by clicking split | **Not trivial mount/dismount**: may be terminated or separated only via legal costly resonance policy; exact deep-merger reversibility **TBD** |
| Infrastructure | Assembly gantry/docks, mechanical joints, energy/heat/parts | Living growth chambers/veins/mitochondria/nourishment/metabolism | Meditation/concentration, actual Energy, resonance and optionally authorized Obelisk links |
| Tactical scale | Mech, combiner, Colossus, giant mechanical Titan | Larger mutated organism, permanent fused predator, possible superorganism | Temporary coordinated Chorus / deep energetic synthesis / potential Obelisk collective |
| Primary cost | Locked component machines not independently available, repair, structural mass, component loss | Permanently consume precursor units and biological material, nutrition and specialization | Energy, meditation time, bandwidth, ability to act independently, resonance stability; irreversible identity consequences **candidate** |
| Counterplay | Attack support joints/heat/power or stop cargo/ammo | Sever supply, force starvation, target biological organs and regeneration costs | Interrupt synchronization, exhaust Energy, split supported graph, challenge vulnerable nodes |

The **identity accounting is essential**. These are not three different button skins for \`combine_units()\`. The legal treatment of source actors and outcomes differs fundamentally.

## MAN / already DEC-046

- An existing machine may transform to a legal posture (UMU tracks / conveyor / bunker / Sentry Wall etc.) without becoming a new actor.
- Several real machines can physically join as a meta-machine, retaining child component IDs, damage, inventory and shared master AI/player/human command authority.
- When disassembled, surviving serviceable constituents can become independent again. They **do not heal or regain lost components automatically**. Locked source parts do no parallel independent mining, transport, energy generation, firing or wall duties while integrated; DEC-041 one UMU active job.
- Future giant mechs and Titans carry military/industrial fantasy inspired conceptually by General Daimos, Transformers and Warhammer 40K; original Scalfagus designs, **no copying recognizable copyrighted robot designs**.
- The owner-approved **4/6/8 UMU undercarriage for small/medium/large mobile buildings** belongs to DEC-041 and is **not** a confirmed formula for a meta-mech.
- Exact recipes, tech tree, sizes, crew skills and energy/heat balance remain open.

## ORG / new owner direction

**Owner:** "Orgi budują jednostki poprzez łączenie na stałe więcej jak jednej lub pożeranie czy pochłanianie. Mutacja transformacja w jedną stronę."

This establishes two broad ways of acquiring new specialized units **without ever giving the MAN-style reversible disassembly of the former organisms**:

**A. Permanent biological fusion (owner-established).** A nonempty compatible group of two or more source organism actors physically and legally arrives at a fusion/incubation site, or other later-approved eligible growth event. The sources **end their separate lives/entities irreversibly**; a new organism is born with a traceable source lineage and real biological resources consumed in transformation. It may inherit traits only according to explicit permitted mutation recipes, not blindly all abilities at no cost.

**B. Devouring / absorption (owner-established).** An existing ORG organism can physically devour/absorb real eligible biomass/organisms and undergo mutation. The source is consumed exactly once; the recipient may be mutated into a new form/receive legal organic traits, requiring actual cost, time and physiological feasibility. This does **not** mean arbitrary hostile machines may be eaten and converted into functioning tank cannons or unrestricted player enemy tech extraction. Which sources are compatible (friendly/enemy/neutral organic life, tissue only or live units), battlefield vs chamber ingestion, and which traits pass are TBD.

**C. Metamorphosis/cocoon (prior ORG irreversible philosophy plus podcast candidate).** An organism transforms itself to a more specialized or higher-order form through biological growth. Older \`units.yaml\` already states irreversible ORG tier upgrades and permanent specializations. The podcast's specific "Łono Mutacji", cocoon ceremony and visual stages remain creative candidates.

**No reversible biological resurrection:** you cannot click DECOMBINE on a large organism and spawn multiple original, fully healthy, original-level units with their exact old HP as though they were physical MAN attachments. Destruction or recycling may yield finite biological loot per existing world rules, **not living precursor restoration**.

**Metabolic rule:** the giant organism has real nourishment demands and links through valid ORG vein systems. Merged organs don't mint free nutrition or remove DEC-012 vascular home requirement. Its HP regen is constrained by real DEC-037 rules, not infinite healing.

**Ownership / experience / uniqueness (candidate implementation):** event ledger records source unit IDs as \`consumed_by_fusion\` or \`consumed_by_absorption\`, one resulting \`offspring_id\` or retained-mutated \`host_id\` depending on recipe, inherited permitted provenance data, existing resources spent and no source cloning. Whether hero/personality/XP persists or is transformed is owner TBD.

## ETH / new owner direction

**Owner:** "Eth są pośrodku nie tak mount i dismount jak mamusi ale mogą energetycznie połączyć się jak org na sale w may złożony byt lub medytując połączyć się odwaga sobie w nowy system"

Interpretation (typos preserved in raw archive): ETH is intermediate and has **two qualitatively different energetic union families**. Unlike MAN, one cannot simply detach physical parts. Unlike ORG, not every participation must necessarily destroy the original entities.

**A. Energetic merger into a complex being — owner direction.**
- Two or more compatible ETH entities may energetically fuse into one more complex resulting being. It is an actual new form, not a free stack of individual HP/shields/DPS.
- Real sources, Energy and identities must be accounted for. The scope of retained identities, memories, unit original HP, fate of sources during deep merge and reversibility after deep synthesis are **open**. Existing ETH single-unit tier ascend/downgrade is stated in \`units.yaml\` as reversible but costly. Do not automatically extend that assertion to all permanent multi-being fusion OR claim all deep unions permanently consume originals.
- Candidate name: **Synteza**; result **Ascendent**. Names are podcast creations and NOT owner ratified.

**B. Meditation-based joined system — owner direction.**
- Several real ETH participants meditate/connect to form **one higher-order system**. Participants can remain physically separate nodes yet share coordinated functions via a legal resonance relationship, real energy budget and permissions.
- A linked "Chór" is a candidate term; **Konstelacja** names a candidate large multi-Obelisk network manifestation. Neither term nor requirements, range or selected number of units are owner-approved.
- If linked via Obelisks, obey canonical tip-to-tip Ether graph, finite throughput/congestion/energy and interruption rules. A system must not use "shared consciousness" to see fogged enemies, teleport physical inventory, mint new Energy or ignore shielding/HP legality.
- Breaking meditation/link should generate explicit failure handling and keep the physical participants' real IDs unless a **deep merger** was legally committed.

**Core gameplay difference:** ETH union is **resonance**: sometimes participants cooperate as a coordinated distributed whole, sometimes deeply merge into a new entity. Reversal is neither free nor assumed fully impossible; exact terms require further owner decision.

**Shared identity theme:** MAN parts remain themselves, ORG source beings are sacrificed into successor life, ETH individuals may share identity or become a new joined being at existential/energy cost.

## Balancing and physical invariants

1. Costs always strictly finite and time positive; no "instant giant" or free resurrecting components.
2. MAN attaches source parts in a physical composite group; ORG consumes originals into one-way successor; ETH either organizes a linked group or synthesizes energetic beings with a distinct identity law. **A common API may support all three, but the transformations of source identities must not be reused blindly**.
3. Common bookkeeping: source and destination actor IDs, cargo and ammo, Energy, HP and shield ledgers, owner, experience/skills, existing garrison, cooldowns, battle class/victory classification; no duplicate resulting units or duplicative components.
4. No transformation bypasses world-space occupancy, range/valid docking, platform edges, fog of war or expected supply continuity.
5. No one-stop auto-win giant. MAN component joints/energy failure, ORG metabolic drain/organ damage, ETH costly and interruptible network/resonance provide meaningful counters.
6. Returning to small units: MAN legal mechanical split; ORG no; ETH mediation-dependent and TBD for deep irreversible/altered consciousness.
7. All novel deep transformation/mega-units belong **after** first DEC-033 MAN mirror MVP0 and should not derail actual game coding/tests.
