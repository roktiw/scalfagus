# Scalfagus — Colony Topology Canon: Organs, Manus, Eters

Date: 2026-10-11
Status: **supplementary detailed analysis under approved DEC-012/DEC-013**; the authoritative combined source is [base ecologies](base-ecologies-and-expansion-2026-10-11.md) and [base-ecologies.yaml](base-ecologies.yaml). Implementation and balance proposals are marked. This document supersedes conflicting portions of earlier logistics/dimensions notes regarding ETH MID/MAX, Organite vascular dependencies and Manus expansion independence.

## Core fantasy: each civilization owns a *different relationship to home*
- **ORG — Organites / Living Organism:** a base *buds outward from a rooted Nexus*. The colony is a connected organism whose structures are nourished by living vascular/mycelial conduits. A remote expansion needs **BOTH** continuous live vascular connectivity to the home Nexus **AND** recurring real nourishment shipments originating at home. Cutting the conduit or blocking home-origin cargo independently causes a staged withering risk; local stock grants only bounded grace. Visually, a timelapse looks like branching blood vessels, not a uniform Zerg-style green creep carpet.
- **MAN — Manusians / Modular Industry:** a base is an *assembled organism of machines*, and most of its purpose-built structures can reposition (walk/crawl on tracks/wheels). It can spread vertically and radially, split into industrial islands, and found **self-sufficient independent expansion bases** with the *lowest entry barrier*. A Manus outpost does NOT have to send periodic supply pings to the original headquarters in order to remain functional. Remote bases remain hidden from enemies only under standard fog-of-war, **never through special unconditional invisibility**.
- **ETH — Etherans / Resonant Knowledge Network:** buildings are *network nodes and advanced vessels*. MID is a reconfigurable **pylon** (storage/handoff, network relay, defensive turret), not the previously proposed only-a-courier MID. MAX is **Nexus-class immediately**, with local command/knowledge/state authority. New remote colonies generally need home-network contact during founding or when requesting architectural patterns/knowledge not held locally; when correctly bootstrapped, an established expansion can keep operating independently until an actual missing blueprint/synchronization is required. Certain specialized structures levitate/reposition after a slow meditation/reconfiguration, not all buildings by fiat.

This is economic/territorial asymmetry, **not** three different physics engines. All factions use Matter, Volatiles, Energy, finite transport, independent locally functional actors and validated orders.

## 1. ORG: Living vascular colonies
### Approved owner intent
- Replace ubiquitous creep/slime tiles with **explicit branching vascular/mycelial organs**: tentacles, vascular roots, fungal tendrils, blood/lymph-like vessels, muscle ducts. Organic aesthetics may be asymmetric or fractal, but **network segments are visually traceable and targetable**.
- Buildings cannot freely pop up beyond their vascular reach. An initial **nourished path must actually extend** from the Nexus/network to where a new bud/structure appears.
- Severed branches **wither rather than instantly explode**: a visible sequence of isolation -> loss of throughput / low reserves -> disabled functions / damage or dormancy -> collapse (exact timers/decay and recovery are proposed).
- ORG expansion must continually **remain rooted through a live vascular path** *and* "knock on home's door" for **recurring physical nourishment deliveries originated from the original home Nexus**. Independent workers can ferry home cargo to sustain a branch temporarily; merely harvesting local resources does not waive the home dependency. Broken conduit still makes the branch structurally disconnected even if a worker brings reserve packets. Reconnect the conduit to restore full viability. No perpetual invisible lifeline.
- All units are **individual specialists**, not a centralized hivemind. Conduits transport nutrient/material/structural support, **not every mind**.
- MIN can grow first-level structures; MID supports/grows second-level structures; MAX/mother supports third-level structures **only if construction capability and nourishment are legal**, not because every large creature is instantly a building.

### Important split: the two ORG networks
**Structural/lifeline graph** (vascular): continuous traceable static/slow-grown conduits enable *building viability and budding*; can be cut and repaired; maintenance consumes actual recorded costs.

**Transport logistics** (mobile cargo): independent small workers and large flying mothers haul **physical packets of Matter, Volatiles, Energy** around sources and living depots. They can physically feed needy branches, but this is not automatic global wallet sharing.

A vessel animation is not free resource teleportation. A vascular edge can be given finite nutrient flow capacity. A deposit packet can be consumed on upkeep, transferred into the branch's local inventory, or converted to nutrient support only via an explicitly specified recipe. No mandatory fourth "biomass" wallet.

### ORG MIN / MID / MAX: building+transport units
| Scale | Role | Builds | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MIN | Fast small gatherer, branch-tender, manual feeder | Tier 1 budding / conduit segments | Cheap, responsive, short range direct nourishment, repairs severed strands | Small cargo, fragile, can be intercepted |
| MID | Armored carrier/harvester, local reservoir and branch caretaker | Tier 2 node/structure where valid | Multi-slot transport and temporary nourishment, flexible local staging | Slower and costlier; baseline one port/item per handoff |
| MAX | **Flying mother** cargo transporter, Tier 3 specialist growth support | Tier 3 when equipped and legal | Big cargo loops, passenger carrying if equipped, can supply distant growth at scale | Very costly to lose, predictable paths, anti-air vulnerability, large silhouette; baseline one transfer port |

**Already-approved DEC-011 stays valid**: unupgraded ORG MID/MAX accept/release *one physical item per completed worker handoff, from one worker at a time*. Unupgraded MAX activity modes MOVE/RECEIVE/DISPATCH/EXTRACT are mutually exclusive. A mother flies from source to base on a **repeating route**, **stops/holds to transfer** in early game; **moving** pickup/drop is a **mid-game researched** upgrade, additional ports only later. Route LOOP and in-flight LOADING are two different features. MAX itself is not the fixed Nexus; the grounded Nexus and vascular structures remain the least mobile faction's core.

### Player strategy options (owner question resolved as design recommendation)
1. **Loop / circulatory convoy — PRIMARY for MAX:** Mother flies source pickup -> main/branch base delivery -> source, repeating. In early game it explicitly stops for a bounded service window. Later may unload/load on the move by research. This is the faction's signature readable movement.
2. **Stationary rendezvous / shuttle feeder — possible MID option:** MID parks near deposit/tendril as buffer and MINs feed it; its storage is finite and *it is not a conveyor belt*. Suitable for short routes, frantic defense and outlying nutrient rescue. No global free storage sharing.
3. **Single giant living depot — deliberately not default:** permanent static MAX near a single source should be situational (siege staging, emergency isolated vein), and lose the unique flying-loop upside; don't give it MAN belt-like throughput while parked.

How to prevent a solved single optimal route: **load/unload port bandwidth, distance, path width, worker availability, cargo occupancy, interception risk and branch upkeep**. A large flying MAX has high capacity but cannot endlessly outrun load bottlenecks. Multiple smaller mothers offer redundancy and faster cycle frequency at higher total overhead. Maintain at least one low-cost MIN-only recovery route.

### Two independent failure conditions (owner direction)
A branch can fail from **(A)** a broken vascular link or **(B)** missed home-origin nourishment deliveries, **even when the link is intact**. Both failures should be separately telegraphed in game and telemetry. Workers may rescue via actual finite food shipments but cannot permanently bypass the structural connection requirement. Implementation detail: source identity may be tracked in a transfer receipt or explicit upkeep token derived from the three canonical resources; do not create a fourth fungible economy resource.

### Concrete starvation prototype (PROPOSED numeric-free state machine)
\`GROWING -> CONNECTED -> ISOLATED_BUFFERED -> STARVING -> DORMANT -> WITHERED\`.
- Disconnect: set connection graph broken immediately; local reserve remains and pays decay/upkeep.
- Workers may physically feed the branch to extend survival or restore function when permitted; valid reconnection heals toward CONNECTED.
- Structures in DORMANT cannot produce/train even if their art still stands; preserve inventory until a documented collapse/destruction rule.
- Optional source outposts must not be an infinite self-feeding loophole unless an explicit future 'independent offshoot' technology is approved; initial design forbids permanent self-sufficient ORG outposts.

## 2. MAN: modular mobile industrial islands
### Approved owner intent
- **MIN:** SCV-like independent worker/operator repairing other machines and buildings, equipped to extract/haul/build.
- **MID:** tracked **physical relay**, with (a) conveyor-belt mode, (b) **one-person bunker** capacity when outfitted/authorized, (c) Sentry Wall mode by turning the belt outward from protected base, physically ejecting its real cargo *behind* the wall, and (d) movement/repositioning. The user explicitly approved the *one-person MID bunker*, which was absent from previous logistics YAML.
- **MAX:** versatile slow ground **mobile warehouse OR multi-soldier bunker**, not a flying craft; finite garrison. Resource crates must be physically unloaded/ejected to legal slots when switching to bunker, never credited invisibly.
- **Nexus:** an assemblage/network of mobile buildings and units, not a compulsory single immobile monument. The complete industrial base can crawl across the map, grow vertically, split into islands, and merge when useful.
- Most **designed** MAN structures should be capable of locomotion/repositioning; this does not mean a generic unit may transform into arbitrary structures. Exposed machinery, wheel/track/leg mechanisms, deployed footprints and checks remain mandatory.
- **Low threshold for independent expansion**: remote autonomous bases do not need to contact headquarters periodically. Enemy scouting under normal fog-of-war determines whether a new base is visible.

### Economy / defense trade-off
\`MOVE -> BELT -> SENTRY_WALL\` for MID plus optional \`ONE_PERSON_BUNKER\` mode (whether bunker and wall may be simultaneous on a single chassis: **OPEN**, default safe proposal mutually exclusive). Every deployment validates clearance, carried cargo, mode permission, time and resource cost. A stopped belt breaks throughput; dumped packets preserve ledger.

\`MOBILE_DEPOT -> DEPLOYED_DEPOT -> MULTI_BUNKER\` for MAX, with actual soldier entities in finite garrison slots, costed switch and no simultaneous full commercial throughput and fortification unless separately researched.

**Spatial signature:** four dispersed workshops are not automatically worse than one big headquarters. Their repair/mobility and individual supply storage need to be paid for. Watchers may discover an isolated outpost by normal fog-of-war scouting, not by forced global reveal.

## 3. ETH: ancient resonant civilization with Nexus-class MAX
### Approved owner intent — REVISION OF PREVIOUS ETH MID/MAX MAPPING
- **MIN:** small autonomous worker/courier/scout. To **build**, it enters NETWORK mode and physically connects with a **visible blue beam** to the closest or otherwise eligible larger node (not an instantaneous Protoss-style energy orb followed by immediate flight away). The beam relays energy/blueprint authorization for a finite duration; building consumes finite Matter/Volatiles/Energy. Opponents with actual line of sight may detect it, with stealth/reveal balance TBD.
- **MID:** **pylon**, able to shift among **STORAGE / NETWORK RELAY / DEFENSIVE TURRET** modes. Mobility of particular MID pylons is conditional on a slow meditation/levitation transformation and legal route; they aren't generic roaming skiffs by default. The old "MID Relay Conductor mobile courier" is **superseded as the default economic MID**; such a craft may still be an optional specialized separate unit.
- **MAX:** **Nexus-class from acquisition/construction**, providing a high-capacity local node and a local library/authority for known patterns. It is not just a large warehouse/pylon waiting to become a Nexus via later upgrade. It can act as anchor for remote expansion after initial contact/bootstrap.
- ETH builds through a mix of *worker action* (MAN analogy) and *network-dependent authorization/transfer* (ORG analogy). Some structures can uproot by levitating **after long meditation**, at real cost and temporary exposure.
- A remote ETH expansion "knocks on home's door" **only when created or when requiring a blueprint/pattern not yet held locally**. No universal continuous nutrition tether.

### ETH construction and visibility
A worker joins NETWORK stance -> ray traces a legal path to **eligible higher node** -> locks a visible channel for duration and consumes resources through actual allowed reservations -> constructing form progresses -> job completes -> beam stops. Neither cargo nor units teleport automatically as a side effect.
**Critical distinction:** a beam isn't unconditional map-wide vision/reveal. Under fair fog-of-war rules enemies who could detect the bright effect gain some intel. Exact interaction (vision through fog, beam visibility through walls, concealment tech, warnings) requires playtest, not assumed.

### ETH mid modes and pressure
\`STORAGE <-> NETWORK <-> MEDITATION <-> TURRET\` is a conceptual family, not necessarily direct one-click every-state transitions. A pylon only does its currently active mode's job at full efficiency. **Proposed continuity of earlier pylon rule:** STORAGE/NETWORK keep finite cargo; during MEDITATION all gates close and the inventory remains sealed; TURRET keeps cargo but throughput is limited/disabled (balance test).
Modes are not free toggles, network cuts produce finite delays/queues, and a MID turret can guard only a finite arc/range. Mobile MID moves only after a costly meditation into \`LEVITATING_MOVE\`, with relay/weapon services disabled. MAX as Nexus does not automatically grant free emergency teleportation or infinite hub capacity.

### ETH autonomy via local knowledge
A new node requires parent sync for permission/patterns to be seeded. Once its MAX is registered and owns the correct pattern set, its builders use local authorized patterns without needing to contact distant original home on every action. Novel/rare tech request requires a bounded synch handshake. "Info" is a **tech prerequisite**, not a mandatory fourth economic currency. No live LLM API dependency or collective mind.

## 4. Cross-faction fairness and player-readable danger
- ORG: continuity risk, starvation, mother interception; strong emergency workers and redundant channels.
- MAN: supply route ambush and isolated island raids; strong independent colonies and highly modular structures, but no free limitless building movement.
- ETH: visible NETWORK beams during building and node-mode lockouts, tech pattern availability; strong local advanced hubs but limited early expansion cadence.
- Every civilization has a MIN fallback to harvest/repair/recover after a high-tier loss. No guaranteed defeat from a single trivial connection cut.
- Asset loss and mode changes must conserve resource quantities, occupancy, garrison persons and work orders; no zero-cost defense plus full-throughput economy.

## 5. Timelapse / telemetry prototype brief
**ORG:** Render branching vascular graph from center; mark branches green/healthy vs dark/brown/starving and show feeding animals physically traversing edges/air loops. Destroy one segment and visibly show one branch decline over time while MIN/MID rescue it.
**MAN:** Render modular buildings walking/crawling across terrain, convoy with tracked belts converting to walls, and an independent island forming without a parent tether. Geometry shows rising layered industrial structures instead of one flat rectangular block.
**ETH:** Render geometric triangulations of nodes, blue build beams that disclose frontier works, occasional glowing meditation when relocating structures, and an expansion Nexus working offline except for a novel-pattern retrieval pulse.

## 6. Tests to write before lock-in
- \`ORG-BUD-01\`: refuse new bud without live vascular path or explicitly valid bootstrap action; no free remote creep tile.
- \`ORG-DECAY-02\`: destroy vascular bridge; branch enters finite buffered/starvation phase, units remain independent; reconnection or paid worker feeding can rescue.
- \`ORG-LOOP-03\`: initial mother requires STOP for handoff; single worker and single item per completed operation; moving handoff rejected before upgrade (DEC-011 preserved).
- \`ORG-RESUPPLY-04\`: cargo counted exactly once when feeding an isolated branch; no perpetual free independent base.
- \`MAN-MID-01\`: tracked 3-/5-cell module supports belt and one-person bunker and costed wall; these modes cannot all run full-speed simultaneously.
- \`MAN-WALL-02\`: sentry wall ejects all finite packets behind defended direction to legal cells/depots; nothing credited to bank for free.
- \`MAN-ISLAND-03\`: expansion produces locally without HQ communication when materials/infrastructure available; remains scoutable under regular fog.
- \`MAN-MOVE-04\`: only explicitly locomotion-equipped buildings relocate; no instant collision-free sliding during production.
- \`ETH-BUILD-01\`: MIN builder enters network, holds visible bounded beam from valid node until build complete; effect respects fog rules.
- \`ETH-PYLON-02\`: MID pylon cycles STORE/RELAY/TURRET and preserves finite cargo; meditation locks required ports; movement requires legal conversion.
- \`ETH-NEXUS-03\`: MAX has Nexus functionality on creation, can seed local patterns and autonomy.
- \`ETH-SYNC-04\`: only novel pattern requires remote sync; existing local buildings continue to work after parent disconnect.
- \`ALL-LEDGER-05\`: no cargo duplication under simultaneous mode switching, destruction and interrupted beam/vascular feeding.

## 7. Decisions and open questions
**Owner-approved:** vascular rather than creep for ORG; buildings require BOTH live vascular connection AND periodic home-origin physical feeding; connected living base is least mobile; most MAN buildings/mobile modules are designed to relocate and can form independent islands; MAN MIN/MID/MAX roles incl 1-person MID bunker; ETH MIN NETWORK beam, MID pylon storage/relay/turret, MAX immediate Nexus, limited meditation movement, expansion sync only on founding/new information.

**Confirmed existing canon:** ORG baseline mother transfer rules and upgrade gating DEC-011; exactly three stored primary resources; individual autonomous unit identities; exclusive resource/cargo integrity.

**Proposed / not locked:** decay timers, local nutrient recipe and finite rescue grace (but no permanent substitute for the conduit), MAN wall/bunker simultaneous modes, exact building locomotion percentage, ETH beam fog reveal policy, ETH MAX alternative high-tier defense, pattern propagation timing, full ETH network movement and numeric balance.
