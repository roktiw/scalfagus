# Scalfagus — ETH / Etherans: Ancient-Alien Folklore as Original Pixel-Art Lineages

Date: 2026-10-11
Status: **speculative fiction art research and design proposals**, NOT canonical claims about extraterrestrial visitors.
Companion: [colony topology and building mechanics](../design/colony-topologies-vascular-modular-resonant-2026-10-11.md), [broader faction art direction](faction-art-direction-and-transport-v0.2-2026-10-11.md).

## Provenance and epistemic boundary
The owner asked to explore beings **named in UFO/contactee mythology** as visual inspiration for Eters. Examples in conversation: "astar seran" (uncertain spelling; likely related to the *Ashtar Sheran* contactee tradition), Greys ("szaraki") and Reptilians ("reptilianie"). The existence of these beings, their visits to Earth and their appearance are **unverified cultural narratives**, not facts established by scientific observation. Do NOT author Scalfagus lore as if real-world extraterrestrial visits were proven. Some imagery derives from existing copyrighted films/games or esoteric narratives; **design new silhouettes and names** rather than importing exact "species" or cultural claims.

A fiction setting may have **multiple phenotypic lineages** inside an Eter civilization without making them independent playable races or a literal hive mind. A "Grey-like" helper can be a biological individual, a specialized artificial shell or an Eter-born minority; actual ontology is worldbuilding TBD.

## What makes ETH distinct from Protoss
- **Ancient earth-like civilizations** reimagined: pyramids, ziggurats, quasi-sphinx platforms, celestial obelisks, stepped plazas; **do not directly copy named real-world religious monuments one-for-one as alien-owned sites**. Prefer respectful abstraction of geometry and astronomical engineering.
- UFO/UAP **folklore motifs** in saucers, dark disk silhouettes, hovering observation orbs, luminescent fields, interstellar travelers, drop capsules and large motherships — *stylized fictional technology*.
- Interstellar-like cosmic scale, vacuum darkness, luminous orbital rings, clean ceramic hulls, deep silhouettes, rare anime/manga-inspired accent colors, simplified 1980s–1990s science fiction animation design.
- Ancient monuments and high technology occupy same scene: stepped structures support floating domes, auroral rings and precision blue-beam construction; the silhouette is not a gold-armored Protoss temple.
- Etherans are recognizable as **individual specialists and societies**, not one spiritual brain. No mandatory virtue/evil tied to skin or shape.

## ETH MIN / MID / MAX — how to draw the new canon
**MIN — worker / courier / beam builder:** a small autonomous entity, at least one biological or cybernetic lineage and optional saucer/disk worker variant. For construction, extend a distinctive **straight/segmented blue beam** from the worker to a *larger valid node*, with visible channeling animation and exposed beam endpoint. The construction site forms in stages at finite resource cost. Crucially, **no Protoss-style sphere dropped on location while worker leaves**.

**MID — pylon** with three clear poses:
- STORAGE: horizontal capacitor petals open, visibly guarded cargo doors and finite slots.
- NETWORK: geometric rings align and a few controlled beam connectors pulse; relay and pattern coordination.
- TURRET: outer petals lock, energy emitter is exposed, cargo gates close or degrade according to balance; attack tells are long enough to counter.
- MOVEMENT where specialized: after meditation it lifts/levitates slowly, with visible transition, input/output disabled while relocating. No generic skiff substituted for the pylon by default.

**MAX — Nexus-class on arrival:** a **pyramid/ziggurat foundation paired with a dome/orb/ring complex**, visually larger, stable local blueprint store and command role. May later carry defensive/stellar technology, but its *initial job is Nexus*, not "only storage until upgrade". Include human-scale entry ports, distinct three-point base footprint and one large orbiting halo or nested domes. When starting an expansion, a bright finite **founding/synchronization beam** can betray location; after local knowledge bootstrap, the remote Nexus functions alone using known patterns.

### Building & environment studies (proposed)
1. **Stepped Array:** flattened obelisk-ziggurat with radar-like ring around summit; network structure.
2. **Sphinx Observatory:** quasi-sphinx silhouette integrated into geometric landing pad and elongated visor-orb (abstract, not the Great Sphinx itself).
3. **Pyramid Hangar:** open triangular prism with floating circular docking platform inside.
4. **Crown Dome:** shallow luminous dome sitting on heavy stepped pedestal; shields/medical stabilization.
5. **Orbit Archive:** several levitating rings around black monolith; blueprint library.
6. **UAP Survey Spire:** dark dish and spherical sensor arrays; range/detection.
7. **Nexus Pyramid:** MAX tier, huge stepped base with luminous oculus and thin concentric halo.

Use **limited recurring golden-ratio/Fibonacci proportional motifs** as art-direction hints, not a mathematical requirement that makes buildings same-shaped or clip on a pixel grid. Allow ceramic whites, deep navy, violet-blue skin and eyes, pale turquoise beams, occasional amber highlights. Preserve readable silhouettes at 320x240.

## Eight-direction technical treatment
- Desired moving-unit coverage: N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW; first playable proof may use **four directions** plus true directional exceptions or explicit mirroring when symmetry supports it.
- Important for asymmetrical Eter clothing/sensors: horizontal mirroring may flip ownership/weapon port and should be art-reviewed.
- Buildings fixed in place do **not** need eight body facing sprites unless rotated by gameplay; a pylon needs **mode** poses instead.
- Faction lineage should survive even with the glow switched off; identify workers by posture, not only eye color.
- ETH construction blue beams and rings are **separate VFX layers** attached to an actual actor/projectile or construction state, not fictional lines disconnected from simulation.

## Gameplay boundaries and perception
- Alien lineages are **character families and differentiated render rigs**, not proof every Eter body shape can shoot or build.
- The beam may disclose coordinates **only to opponents who satisfy an actual detection/visibility rule**. The owner has deliberately deferred exact stealth/reveal balance.
- MAX local knowledge archives are prerequisites; "information" is not automatically a fourth economic resource.
- No automatically reading enemy minds; no racial omniscience.
- Flyers, motherships and orbital structures require real collision/LOS/landing/docking and Energy costs; no free line-of-sight through fog.

## Recommended first three sprite studies
1. **Orbital Surveyor / MIN** — 8-direction move plus 4-channel beam build animation; 24x32 or 32x48 source depending on tested readability.
2. **MID Pylon** — STORAGE/NETWORK/TURRET/MEDITATION static poses, with one slow LEVITATION transition variant if a deployable moves.
3. **Nexus Pyramid / MAX** — three-quarter isometric footprint, stepped base, separated halo FX, founding beam endpoint, readable at 320x240.

A fourth study should compare one Saurian Sentinel fighter with one Harmonic Envoy architect in silhouette-only grayscale. This determines whether multiple physiognomies still look like one coherent civilization.
